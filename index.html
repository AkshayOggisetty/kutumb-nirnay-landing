The problem

Vocational education does not fail for lack of information.

It fails because the parent holds the veto and has never been given anything to change their mind with. Every tool on the market talks to the student. Nobody is in the room with the person actually deciding.

01

The parent decides

Students default to the course their parents name. Those parents are working from a labour market they last saw thirty years ago.

02

Status, not evidence

A trade is read as a last resort and a degree as a safe bet — a ranking no one has ever tested against placement rates or wages.

03

Nobody to ask

One counsellor per three thousand students. For most families the conversation simply never happens.

04

And it decays

Even good guidance fades. Aspiration effects measured in trials are largely gone within a fortnight — so one session was never going to work.

How it works

Six steps, and the household is in all of them

Not an aptitude test that produces a PDF. A conversation that ends in a plan both sides have signed.

01

Start with the household, not the student

District, income, the student's stage — and the objection actually being raised at home. Every recommendation is conditioned on what the family can afford to wait for.

02

Show one decision in two views

The student sees what the work is and where it leads. The parent sees when the money starts, what it costs, and how often it ends in a job.

03

Model the household's own cash flow

Fees out, stipend and wages in, discounted by real placement rates. A family is making a financial decision under uncertainty — so show it that way.

04

Map what is actually reachable

Direction, road distance, travel mode, journey time and monthly fare to every ITI, polytechnic, skill hub and college in range. A path is only real if the child can get there every morning.

05

Answer the objection, not the question

“It is a dead end.” “What will people say?” Each one answered with evidence and with the neighbours who already took that route.

06

End with something on paper

A decision record with concerns named, numbers attached, and a review date — because the conversation has to continue after the room empties.

What's different

The recommender already exists. The conversation does not.

National skilling platforms already carry thousands of courses with AI-matched recommendations for students. That problem is solved. This is the other half.

Every career tool today

  • Speaks to the student
  • Starts from a psychometric test
  • Produces a report, once
  • Quotes a salary with no date attached
  • Built for a city, in English
against

Kutumb Nirnay

  • Speaks to the parent holding the veto
  • Starts from the household's economics
  • Stays through the decision window
  • Shows month-by-month household position
  • Voice-first, in the language spoken at home
Reachability

Is it close enough to go every morning?

Bearing gives direction, radius gives distance. Every institute in range, with the travel mode, the journey time and what the commute costs a month — computed, not guessed. Because the best course in the state is worth nothing if the bus does not go there.

Drawn rather than tiled, so it works on a weak connection and a cheap phone.
