Why it matters

Vocational education does not fail because families lack information.

It fails because the person holding the decision has never been given anything solid enough to change their mind with. Four things get in the way, and every one of them is fixable.

01

The parent decides, and nobody is talking to them

Students take the course their parents name. Those parents are working from a labour market they last saw decades ago, and no career tool on the market addresses them directly.

02

Status stands in for evidence

A trade reads as a last resort and a degree as a safe bet. That ranking has never been tested against what each one pays, or how often either ends in a job. Put the two side by side and the ranking often reverses.

03

There is no one to ask

One counsellor serves roughly three thousand students. For most families the conversation simply never happens, so the decision gets made on rumour and on whatever the neighbours did.

04

Even good advice fades

Research on aspiration tracks how quickly encouragement wears off, often within a fortnight. A single counselling session was never going to be enough, which is why this is built to stay with a family through the whole admission season.

How it works

Four steps, and the household is part of every one

No aptitude quiz that produces a report nobody reads. A guided conversation that ends in a plan the student and the parent have both seen and agreed to.

01

Start with the household, not a personality test

Where you live, what the household earns, how soon another earner is needed, and what the student actually enjoys. Options are then ranked for your situation rather than in the abstract, because a three year course means something very different on nineteen thousand a month than on sixty.

02

Compare two options the way each person sees them

The student gets the course content, the qualification level and where it can lead. The parent gets the fees, the month earning begins, the starting pay and how often that course actually ends in a job. Same screen, both answers.

03

See the household balance, month by month

Fees going out, stipend and wages coming in, adjusted for how often each course leads to work. You get the month the household breaks even and the point where one option overtakes the other, instead of a salary figure with no date attached.

04

Find the centres you can genuinely reach

Every institute near you with the road distance, how to travel, the journey time and what the commute costs each month. The best course in the state is worth nothing if there is no bus that goes there.

What makes it different

The course recommender already exists. The family conversation does not.

National skilling platforms already list thousands of courses and match them to students. That part is solved. This is the half nobody has built.

Reachability

Close enough to go every morning?

Direction around the circle, distance out from the centre. Every institute in range carries the road distance, the way to travel, the journey time and the monthly fare, all worked out rather than guessed. Anything beyond daily travelling distance is marked clearly, so hostel costs go into the plan before a course is shortlisted.

The map is drawn rather than loaded from a tile service, so it opens quickly on a slow connection and keeps working when the signal does not.

Your family details never leave your phone

There is no account to create and no server storing your answers. Everything you enter stays in your own browser, and the printed plan is generated on your device. When you are finished you can clear it with a single tap.
